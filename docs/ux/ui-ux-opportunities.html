  • Four defects, now fixed. The Audit log could not filter Company Assets. "All N waiting" opened Orders on the wrong tab. The time after Mark as Ordered was in the browser's time zone. Orders forgot its filters after View Order and Print Order.
  • Administration has none of the new patterns yet. No IssueBadge, InfoTip, HelpPopover, DisclosureSection or RuleList. Its figures lead nowhere, its badges do not explain themselves, and its hints are paragraphs.
  • The staff app's other panes lag the item pane. In the order pane the actions sit under the lines and the facts run together on one line. The employee page is boxed. Hints are paragraphs that phones hide.
  • The biggest saving is a shared list-and-detail view. Five screens copy it. After it come filter tabs (copied 9 times), the loading/error ladder (about 25), action errors (8, plus 13 confirms) and a link style (about 20). The link style also fixes 12 links that have no focus ring.

Suggested order and decisions

  1. Defects D1–D4. Done.
  2. Quick wins, effort S each: S3, S4, S10, S12, S15, S16, A1, A2, A8, A10, A11, plus X5 (links without a focus ring) and X7 (double submit).

  5. The list-and-detail view X1. Then S17 (Employees beside the list) and the admin panes on it.
  6. New capabilities that need the API or bigger flows: S11, S13, S14, S18, A4, A7 and A13.

Decisions for you

Given as a green badgeS5 and X10. The guidelines say green means done. Today Given is black, and Signed is the green one.
Employees beside the listS17. The employee page would become a pane on wide screens, like Orders and the catalogue.
Replacing a confirmation linkS11. Keep replacing it silently, or ask first and show the old link's state.
One count style on filter tabsX2. The plain muted number most screens use, or the pill the registers use.
