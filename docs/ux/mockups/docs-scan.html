Scan Documents

A phone app that photographs a passport or ID card, reads it (the MRZ and the printed fields), finds the person among the employees and updates, merges or adds them, then files the photos in the personnel file. It looks and moves like the iPhone's Camera and Photos. Everything inside the phone can be tapped. The people and documents are invented specimens.
