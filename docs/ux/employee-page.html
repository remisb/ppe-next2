The employee's page: where people reach it from

An employee today is only ever a pane beside the Employees list, wherever the link was. Four ways to add a full page and decide which link opens what, drawn as flows and as screens, and a recommendation: Option B with A, the list keeps its pane, every other link opens the page, and the pane can open it too.

20 Nov 2026 · build f247d36 · code review of the staff app · text and rules: employee-page.md · builds on record-actions

1. How the app reaches an employee today

One address, /employees/<id>, and it is always the Employees list with the employee beside it (22–30rem from lg, the whole screen below). Nine of the ten ways in are a person looking for one employee; only the list itself is someone looking through employees.

F1 · Too narrow

The app's largest record (sizes, profile, photos, Replacements due, Items given, Ordered, SIM cards, Equipment, Identity, Changes) in a 30rem column. Items given stacks; a year of orders is 2–4 screens of one column.

F2 · Lands on a list

From an order, the employee's name switches the section to Employees and draws a list nobody asked for. Her ← says Back to Employees and goes there, not to the order.

F3 · Two rules

SIM cards and Equipment already open beside their register and as a page from elsewhere (/assets/<id>, one AssetDetail with place). Employees have only the pane.

2. Options

A · Open page from the pane

list→pane— ⤢ Open page →page
order, SIM card, ⌘K→pane(as today)
  • Smallest change; the person chooses when the pane is too small
  • Gmail's, Linear's "open in full"
  • F2 stays: links still land on the list, ← still goes to it
  • Two clicks to see someone in full from an order

B · The page from everywhere but the list Recommended, with A

list→pane— ⤢ →page
order, SIM card, Replacements, dashboards, ⌘K→page
page— ← Back to WE-000010 →where they came from
  • Fixes F1 and F2; the way back returns the person
  • Same rule as SIM cards and Equipment: a register opens beside itself, everything else opens the page
  • The list keeps J / K and merging
  • Two places draw the record: one component with place keeps them equal
  • A new address

C · The page replaces the pane

list→page— ← Employees →list
  • One place for the record
  • Loses J / K and the list beside the open one: checking ten people's sizes is ten round trips
  • Breaks list-and-detail for one list only

D · Tabs on the page

OverviewItemsAssetsDocumentsChanges

A way through the page, not a way in; goes with A, B or C.

  • A short first screen
  • Hides parts; Ctrl+F misses closed tabs; a phone needs a scrolling tab row
  • Closed, counted sections already give the short first screen

6. Rollout and open questions

Rollout

  1. EmployeeDetail out of routes/employee.tsx, with place; the pane uses it unchanged.
  2. The route employeePage at /employees/<id>/page; section Employees; ← through back() with a from label.
  3. Every link but the list's own to the page; ⌘K's employees too.
  4. ⤢ Open page in the pane: actionIcons.page, a tool in employeeActions.
  5. The page's two columns.
  6. e2e: from an order to the page and back; Open page; 375, 1024, 1440px in EN, LT, RU.
  7. Then catalogue items, if it holds up.

Open questions

  1. The address: /employees/<id>/page, or another?
  2. The list's names: href the page (a middle-click opens it in a new tab), a plain click the pane?
  3. The Employee Dashboard's links: the page, as everywhere else?
  4. Catalogue items next, or employees only?
  5. Print the page as a one-person summary?
