Order ER diagram

The tables of the Order aggregate (internal/domain/order): the order, its immutable snapshot lines and its confirmations, with the live tables they point at. Built from migrations 0006, 0007, 0009, 0013, 0015, 0021 and 0027. The rules behind the columns are in order-service.md; the same diagram in Markdown is order-er-diagram.md.

employees, catalogue_items and users are shown by their keys only. Nothing an order or its record shows is read from them: names, prices and sizes are copied in at Mark as Ordered.
