Equipment ER diagram

The tables behind Equipment & Furniture in Company Assets (internal/domain/asset, ADR 0004): the item, its assignments and their signed copies, its inventory numbers, and the role that lets a user see it. An item is an assets row with kind = 'EQUIPMENT'; SIM cards share the tables (sim-card-er-diagram.html). Built from migrations 0029_company_assets, 0030_asset_signed_copies and 0032_equipment_assignments_role. The rules behind the columns are in asset-service.md ("Equipment & Furniture"); the same diagram in Markdown is equipment-er-diagram.md.

Only the columns an item uses are shown: the SIM card's sim_no, phone_no, provider, plan, connection_status and received_date are always null for one, so equipment has no status. employees and users are shown by their keys only. Location, the holder and Days Held are not columns: they are derived from the open assignment. An item's Changes are audit_events rows with entity_type = 'asset' and its id, which no foreign key ties to it.
