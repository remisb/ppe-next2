SIM Card ER diagram

The tables behind a SIM card in Company Assets (internal/domain/asset, ADR 0004): the card, its assignments and their signed copies, its inventory numbers, and the Settings columns Add SIM and Give SIM fill in from. A SIM card is an assets row with kind = 'SIM'; Equipment & Furniture share the tables (equipment-er-diagram.html). Built from migrations 0029_company_assets, 0030_asset_signed_copies, 0031_default_sim_provider, 0033_sim_providers, 0034_default_sim_non_return_value and 0035_default_sim_plan. The rules behind the columns are in asset-service.md; the same diagram in Markdown is sim-card-er-diagram.md.

Only the columns a SIM card uses are shown: category, name and serial_no are always null for one. employees and users are shown by their keys only. Location, the holder and Days Held are not columns: they are derived from the open assignment. A card's Changes are audit_events rows with entity_type = 'asset' and its id, which no foreign key ties to it.
